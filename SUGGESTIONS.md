# Further Exploration

After you work through the lessons in this repository, consider diving into these areas to expand your statistical and machine learning knowledge.

## Statistical Techniques

- Bayesian inference and probabilistic programming (PyMC or Stan)
- Survival analysis for modeling time-to-event data
- Hierarchical and multilevel models
- Generalized linear models and regularization
- Nonparametric tests and resampling methods
- Advanced time series models such as ARIMA and state-space approaches

## Machine Learning

- Neural networks and deep learning fundamentals
- Support vector machines and kernel methods
- Model interpretability tools such as SHAP or LIME
- Dimensionality reduction with PCA and t-SNE
- Time series forecasting with models like Prophet
- Deployment strategies for putting models into production

## Experimentation and Causal Inference

- A/B testing and design of experiments
- Causal inference with techniques like difference-in-differences
- Power analysis for determining sample sizes

## Additional Resources

- Online courses from Coursera, edX, and university programs
- Books like _An Introduction to Statistical Learning_ and _Bayesian Data Analysis_
- The scikit-learn and statsmodels documentation
- Open datasets from Kaggle or the UCI Machine Learning Repository
- Blogs and communities such as Towards Data Science and r/MachineLearning

These resources provide natural next steps after completing the material in this repository.
